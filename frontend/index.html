How to use

  1. Allow camera permission.
  2. Click START CAMERA.
  3. Confirm your face and hands are visible.
  4. Select one of the 50 INCLUDE-50 signs.
  5. Click CAPTURE SIGN.
  6. Perform the selected sign naturally.
  7. Wait until 45 frames are collected.
  8. Review prediction, confidence and reference match.
